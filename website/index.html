❓ FAQ

Does SandaScan work without an internet connection?

Yes! SandaScan runs completely offline. No data ever leaves your computer.

Why is my searchable PDF not searchable?

You need Tesseract OCR installed. Download from github.com/UB-Mannheim/tesseract/wiki, then run: pip install pytesseract

Can I process multiple pages at once?

Yes. Add all your images or an entire folder, then click Process All. They will be merged into a single multi-page PDF.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, JPEG, PNG, TIFF, TIF, and BMP.

Will SandaScan alter my original images?

No. Your original files are never modified. The app creates new PDF output.

Does SandaScan use AI to generate text?

No. SandaScan uses computer vision — not AI generation. It preserves every pixel faithfully, never rewriting or inventing text.

What DPI should I use?

300 DPI is recommended for most uses. Use 600 DPI for archival/master copies.

Is SandaScan free?

Yes. SandaScan is open-source under the MIT License.
